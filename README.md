## Overview

Large Language Models (LLMs) are increasingly used for information retrieval, question answering, scientific writing, and knowledge-intensive tasks. However, these models can generate statements that are incorrect, unsupported, or misleading.

This repository focuses on the evaluation of factual accuracy and factuality of LLMs, particularly in scientific domains.

It collects research papers, benchmarks, datasets, tools, implementations, and learning resources that can help researchers understand and evaluate factuality, hallucination, claim verification, and evidence-grounded generation.
